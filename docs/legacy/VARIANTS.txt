READsparrow v0.12 observed-variant reporting

count-flex retains exact v0.11 behavior. count-variants uses the same complete
adapter placement and exact spacer acceptance, then reports refused nonexact
spacers separately. Sequence groups in sequence_counts.tsv remain exact only.
No reference is truncated or deduplicated by dropping IDs; all duplicate ID
membership is retained and not allocated. Candidate groups are hypothetical
reference parents, not known recovered reads.

Hamming search is exhaustive through radius2 using three disjoint literal
blocks. No radius2 neighbor is not a global nearest-distance calculation.
Ns are explicit and not imputed. Unique Hamming1 variants expose a parent
exact count and observed/(observed+parent) ratio; this ratio is descriptive,
not a mutation frequency corrected for sequencing/PCR/library effects.

Candidate C is inherited unchanged from the v0.11 audit. It is evaluated only
for exact-adapter reads with complete quality-supported unique Hamming1
spacers and no other radius2 reference. Different-group offset +/-1..3,
reverse-complement and L-1/L+1 single-indel representations disqualify it.
Same-group indel representations do not resolve mechanism or duplicate IDs.
Changed-base Q<=20 and all other spacer bases Q>=20 are fixed. Q is encoded
Phred+33, not independently measured molecular truth. Gates cannot exclude
all genuine near-reference molecules. In particular a frequent high-quality
variant can contain low-quality observations that also satisfy C.

Observed strings have independent content-derived VAR identifiers and are
never silently rewritten into reference SEQ identifiers. Do not sum variant
or candidate counts into exact counts without a separately validated analysis.
Do not treat DNA groups or recurrent variants as independent guides or neutral
biological controls. No gene-level FDR or hit-calling accuracy follows from
count agreement, assignment rate or constructed read-truth calibration.
